### 6. Critical Requirements & Restrictions

**APPLICATION TIMING:**
- Tokyo wards: MUST apply BEFORE construction starts
- Most municipalities: First-come, first-served basis
- Budget-dependent: Many programs terminate when budget exhausted

**BUNDLING REQUIREMENTS:**
- **Aichi pattern:** Often require multiple equipment (solar + HEMS + battery)
- **Kyoto pattern:** Multiple program tiers with varying requirements
- **Kanagawa pattern:** Prefecture coordinates with municipalities
- **Tokyo pattern:** Can combine city + prefecture + national programs

**COMMON RESTRICTIONS:**
- Battery alone rarely eligible; usually requires solar installation
- Some municipalities require local contractor usage
- Pre-application documentation often required
- Tax payment status verification common

### 7. National Program Backup (Applicable to ALL cities)

Even cities without municipal subsidies have access to:

1. **給湯省エネ2026事業** (Water Heating Efficiency 2026)
   - 70,000-140,000 yen per household
   - Covers Eco-cute, hybrid, fuel cell systems

2. **みらいえエコ住宅2026事業** (Miraieco Housing 2026)
   - Up to 800,000 yen for existing retrofits
   - New construction GX-oriented housing support

3. **先進的窓リノベ2026事業** (Advanced Window Renovation 2026)
   - Up to 1,000,000 yen per project
   - Window/door/insulation improvements

4. **住宅省エネ2026キャンペーン** (Housing Energy Efficiency 2026 Campaign)
   - Comprehensive energy efficiency support
   - Variable by prefecture/municipality
